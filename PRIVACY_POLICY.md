# Privacy Policy for LiveDOM

Last updated: November 2026

LiveDOM ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** LiveDOM does not collect, track, log, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, or user data.
- **Local Settings:** The extension uses Chrome's local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`) strictly to remember your settings (such as inspector mode toggle and activation preferences). This data never leaves your device.
- **In-Memory Edits:** All DOM modifications, CSS tweaks, and HTML edits occur entirely locally inside your browser session and are discarded when the tab is closed, unless you explicitly click "Copy CSS" or "Save HTML".

## 2. Network Activity and Third Parties
- LiveDOM does not connect to any external servers, third-party analytics platforms, or telemetry services.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade any information with third parties.

## 3. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
